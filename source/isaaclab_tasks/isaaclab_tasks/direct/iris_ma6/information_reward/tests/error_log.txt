ITERATION 5 - 2026-06-13 (Slice B: singular-FIM crash in live training)
================================================================================
t050b training crashed at step ~60: torch.linalg.inv (batch 826) singular at sigma_minus.
Root cause (two parts): (1) leave-one-out via SUBTRACTION (fim - info_terms[i]) caused float
cancellation when an agent is near the target (w huge ~1e10) -> prior lost -> singular;
(2) even with masked-sum LOO, a near-target bearing makes the FIM PD but ill-conditioned
(~w/prior ≈ 1e8) which float32 inv reports singular.
Fix: (a) leave-one-out via masked SUM of other agents' terms (no subtraction);
(b) invert in float64 (cast .double() -> inv -> back); (c) reg=1e-6*I; (d) r floor 1e-3->0.5m.
Added test 9 (near-target degenerate geometry). Result: 9/9 pass.
