## 4. Analysis

### Root cause: LR collapse from wider action space + KL-adaptive scheduler

The LR collapse occurs at exactly step 20k — when agent velocity ramp, safety penalty,
and tracking difficulty all activate. This is the same curriculum onset that 928b handled
fine (LR went 0.001→0.0006, a gentle 1.7x drop).

**Why 3461 gets a KL spike but 928b didn't**: the wider action limits (2x yaw, 2x gimbal,
4x zoom) plus different controller dynamics (PX4-matched) mean the same normalized [-1,1]
policy output produces 2-4x larger physical effects. When curriculum difficulty increases
at 20k, these amplified actions create larger reward swings per unit of policy change →
higher KL per update → scheduler slashes LR → floor.

In 928b (45 deg/s yaw, 180 deg/s gimbal, 1x zoom), the same curriculum transition produced
smaller KL, so the scheduler decayed gracefully.

**Evidence this is the action space, not the new features**: all new features (detector
replicator, burst dropout, domain randomization) are curriculum-gated and completely
inactive during 0-40k. The only differences active in the 0-40k window are:
- Controller gains (PX4-matched vs tuned)
- Action limits (2-4x wider)
- Observation dimension (31 vs 30)
- Action zoom weight (0.5 vs 0.3)

### The collapse is not from curriculum length or structure

The curriculum schedule is identical to 928b. The problem is purely the scheduler's
reaction to the larger per-update KL in the wider action space. This can be addressed
through MAPPO hyperparameters without changing the curriculum.

## 5. Decision

**Diagnosis**: KL-adaptive scheduler overreacts at curriculum onset due to wider action space
amplifying per-update KL beyond the 0.02 threshold.

**Next run**: Apply two hyperparameter changes (config-only, no code changes):

```yaml
# Fix 1: Higher LR floor so policy can still adapt after KL spike
min_lr: 3.0e-4          # was 1.0e-4

# Fix 2: Fewer epochs per update to reduce cumulative KL
learning_epochs: 4       # was 6
```

**Rationale**:
- `min_lr=3e-4`: Even if the scheduler floors, 3x more learning capacity than before.
  928b's LR oscillated 0.0002-0.001 through most of training; 3e-4 is within that range.
- `learning_epochs=4`: KL accumulates across epochs. Reducing 6→4 cuts cumulative KL
  by ~33% per update. PPO typically saturates gains after 3-4 epochs anyway.
- If this is insufficient, next lever is `kl_threshold` 0.02→0.03 (wider dead zone).
  928b showed 0.04 caused sigma divergence, but 0.03 may be the sweet spot for the
  wider action space.
- Curriculum extension is **not needed** — the onset timing is fine, only the scheduler
  response needs tuning.

**Alternative not taken**: reducing action limits back to 928b values. Rejected because
the wider limits are needed for PX4 SITL parity and real-world deployment.
