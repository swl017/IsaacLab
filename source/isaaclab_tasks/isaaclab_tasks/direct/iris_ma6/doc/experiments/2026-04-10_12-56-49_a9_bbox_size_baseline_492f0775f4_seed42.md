## 4. Analysis

### What Option A achieved
- **LR**: Perfectly healthy throughout (0.001-0.010). `kl_threshold=0.03` fully resolved
  the LR collapse from 3461 and 949d. This is the validated scheduler configuration.
- **Sigma growth**: Better trajectory than 949d (+0.004/kstep vs +0.001/kstep). The 6
  epochs provide meaningful entropy gradient pressure. The sigma recovery from 0.17→0.55
  shows the mechanism works — it just needs more time.

### What Option A didn't achieve
- **Sigma at noise onset**: 0.48 at 100k. Need ~0.8-1.0+ for noise robustness.
  Extrapolating +0.004/kstep, sigma would reach 0.8 at ~180k — 80k steps too late.
- **The wider action space fundamentally slows sigma growth**. With 2-4x physical
  authority, the policy achieves high reward with narrow sigma. 928b reached 1.22
  at 80k with gentle dynamics; this run only reached 0.44 at the same step.

### The structural problem: noise onset timing vs sigma growth rate

In 928b (gentle dynamics): sigma reached 1.22 by 80k → noise at 100k survived.
In 492f (wide dynamics): sigma reached 0.48 by 100k → noise at 100k fatal.

The sigma growth rate in the wide action space is ~3x slower than 928b's.
No hyperparameter fix will close this gap at the current noise onset timing.

## 5. Decision

**Validated**: `kl_threshold=0.03` + `learning_epochs=6` + `min_lr=3e-4` is the correct
MAPPO configuration for the wide action space. LR behavior is ideal.

**Root cause confirmed**: sigma grows too slowly to survive noise onset at 100k.
The fix must either:
1. **Delay noise onset** — give sigma more time (next experiment: compressed curriculum)
2. **Accelerate sigma growth** — higher entropy coefficient (risky, untested)

**Next experiment**: Compress the non-FP/FN curriculum 2x (all phases start/end halved,
min 20k) so the core curriculum finishes at ~120k. Delay FP/FN to 100k-200k with a
gentle 100k ramp. This gives sigma the entire 0-100k window to grow under pure
difficulty ramps before any detection corruption.
