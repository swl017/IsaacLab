## 4. Analysis

### Noise, not FP/FN, is the primary killer

At 108k: fp_fn_scale = 0.14, noise_scale = 0.40. The degradation pattern
(pair_valid 0.71→0.65→0.54→0.10) tracked noise progression, not FP/FN.

Even with FP/FN on a 200k ramp (barely 14% at the collapse point), the noise
ramp alone at 100k-120k collapsed the policy at sigma=0.48.

### Comparison across runs

| Run | FP/FN ramp | pair_valid at 120k | Root cause |
|-----|-----------|-------------------|------------|
| 492f | 100-120k (20k) | 0.15 | Noise + FP/FN concurrent |
| 025af4d | 100-120k (20k) | 0.20 | Noise + FP/FN concurrent |
| **82364** | **100-300k (200k)** | **0.54** | **Noise alone sufficient** |

The 200k FP/FN ramp delayed the full collapse by ~20k steps but couldn't prevent it.
The noise ramp (always 100k-120k) was the constant factor across all three failures.

### The sigma-action-space link

2be3 survives identical noise onset at sigma=0.12 in normalized space because its
action space is narrower (45°/s yaw, 180°/s gimbal) and penalties are heavier
(-30/-15). The wider action space in all post-2be3 runs produces sigma stagnation
at 0.42-0.48, which is insufficient for noise robustness.

## 5. Decision

**Curriculum shaping cannot fix this.** The noise ramp alone — without any FP/FN — is
enough to collapse the policy at sigma=0.48. No curriculum adjustment to FP/FN timing
changes this.

The problem is upstream: the wider action space + lighter penalties produce a policy
whose sigma is too low to survive any observation corruption.

**Next step**: Full revert to 2be3 configuration (action space, penalties, MAPPO
hyperparams) to verify the training curve matches 2be3. Then re-introduce changes
one at a time to isolate which factor causes sigma stagnation.
