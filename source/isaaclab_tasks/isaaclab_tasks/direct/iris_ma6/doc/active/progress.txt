Continued:
- Corrected stability analysis: K*dt < 2 is the true limit (not K*dt < 1)
  - K*dt < 1: monotonic convergence
  - 1 < K*dt < 2: oscillatory but convergent (pole in (-1, 0))
  - K*dt > 2: unstable (diverges). Confirmed by sweep: K≈220 diverges.
- Swept gains 90-1000 with direct state setting: optimal K=112 (score 5.83 deg)
- Switched to implicit actuator control (PD via set_joint_position_target):
  - Optimal K dropped from 112 → 30.5 (score 5.80 deg — same quality)
  - PD actuator adds natural damping, lowering the required controller gain
  - One-step actuation lag changes effective system order (1st → 2nd)
- Enabled feedback_blend=0.05 to correct internal state drift with implicit actuator
- Final tuned values: pointing_gain=32.5, feedback_blend=0.05, implicit actuator

2026-03-20 — CBF penalty diagnosis + safety curriculum fix
----------------------------------------
Done:
- Diagnosed why 24D-obs runs (c34b3854b1, 0be8d13f02) learn 3-5x slower than
  18D-obs baseline (5ff6c2b6db) in 0-20k steps:
  - Root cause: CBF penalty is 10-100x larger in 24D runs
    (0be8: -0.64 vs 5ff6: -0.09 at step 20k)
  - Safety/collision rate also 2-3x higher in 24D runs at step 2k (0.55 vs 0.26)
  - The 24D obs include ang_vel_b + lin_acc_b which are initially noisy/large,
    causing erratic early exploration that triggers more collisions
  - The massive CBF penalty drowns out bbox_center and bbox_size reward signal
- Key insight: 5ff6 had safety ramping 0-20k but the lower collision rate meant
  CBF penalty stayed negligible (-0.01 to -0.09). With noisier 24D obs, the same
  safety ramp generates penalties 10x larger, dominating the reward.
- Fix: Moved safety_start_step from 0 → 60k, safety_end_step from 20k → 100k
  (commit 11af0e2a72). CBF penalty is now zero during the critical 0-60k bbox
  learning phase, matching the coordination curriculum phase.
- Updated curriculum plot script with per-agent dropout offset fix
- Added backlog doc: unified_randomization_architecture.md (declarative config proposal)
- Experiment runs this session:
  | Commit     | Experiment ID        | Obs | Safety  | Status           |
  |------------|----------------------|-----|---------|------------------|
  | 5ff6c2b6db | initial_difficulty   | 18D | 0-20k   | Good early, collapse 20k+ |
  | c34b3854b1 | initial_difficulty   | 24D | 0-20k   | Slow early (CBF) |
  | 0be8d13f02 | obs_dim_delay_noise  | 24D | 0-20k   | Slow early (CBF) |
  | 11af0e2a72 | obs_dim_safety_fix   | 24D | 60-100k | Running          |
- Policy std divergence identified: 24D runs hit std=1.3 by 10k steps
  (5ff6 reaches 1.3 at 30k). Faster entropy increase suggests the policy
  struggles to extract signal from the noisier observation space.
